## How I Will Add It

Future case studies will follow the strict three-part evidence framework:

1. **Problem**: Identify the specific research problem or engineering limitation being addressed. Clarify why the challenge matters in production or academia without speculative claims.
2. **What I Did**: Detail my exact technical contribution, system architecture, methodology, machine learning models, algorithms, and key decisions made during implementation.
3. **What Came Of It**: Document only verifiable outcomes—such as empirical benchmark results, peer-review milestones, working prototypes, or open-source packages. No unconfirmed metrics or publication venues are ever invented.
