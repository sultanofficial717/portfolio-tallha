# Talha Rehman — Research-Minded AI Engineer Portfolio

Personal portfolio and interactive AI agent for **Talha Rehman** (BS Artificial Intelligence @ Bahria University Islamabad, Founder @ HAT Technologies, Lead @ Tech Fusion / AI Club, and AI Researcher).

This repository contains a high-performance single-page portfolio with an embedded zero-hallucination interactive AI agent, live verification terminal, and deep case studies across AI research, computer vision, and cryptographic scheme design.

---

##  Key Features

- **Interactive Personal AI Agent (`talha-portfolio-agent`)**: A retrieval-only client-side agent with strict zero-hallucination guardrails, first-person verified answers, suggested query chips, and factual citations.
- **Overall Experience & Event Gallery (`gallery.html`)**: Dedicated standalone gallery page with category filtering (Events & Community, AI Research & Labs, Agency & Systems, Journey & Badges) and fullscreen interactive lightbox for milestones, hackathons, and certifications.
- **Terminal Verification Log (`verification_log.sh`)**: Live animated terminal log verifying academic credentials (BS AI @ Bahria University), research manuscripts, internships, and community leadership.
- **Four Deep Research Case Studies**:
  1. *DSPD: Device-Bound Stateless Password Derivation Scheme* (Argon2id, HKDF-SHA256, Random Oracle Model, Elsevier Major Revision, PyPI Package)
  2. *AutismLens* (Multimodal AI System for Manual-Grounded Autism Assessment Interpretation, FYP-I Proposal)
  3. *Brazilian Energy News NLP & CMO-PLD Price-Gap Analytics* (FAST-NUCES, ~30,350 Processed Docs, Gemini Topic Labeling, FinBERT Sentiment)
  4. *Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation (Multimodal RAG)* (Cradle Labs, Cross-Modal Retrieval, 30+ Surveyed Papers, Under Review)
- **Technical Skills Matrix & CV Proficiency Tiers**: Grouped into Expert, Proficient, and Familiar tiers plus 6 domain cards.
- **Track Record & Experience**: Cradle Labs, CEAI (Medical Foot CV), FAST-NUCES (Energy NLP), HAT Technologies, Tech Fusion / AI Club, and U-Capacity.
- **Certifications & Community Leadership**: Anthropic AI Fluency, [Google AI Essentials & Badges on Credly](https://www.credly.com/users/talha-rehman.aa1b925e/badges/credly), LinkedIn Learning, Tech Fusion 2026, and AISeekho Builders Day shortlist.
- **Responsive & Accessible**: Clean fluid typography, mobile drawer navigation, active scrollspy, and floating scroll-to-top control.
